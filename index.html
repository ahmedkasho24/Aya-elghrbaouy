    The faster the blue color appears, the higher the nitrite concentration.

    Why does the color change?

    In experiment 1

    In an acidic medium, nitrite reacts with sulphanilic acid to form a diazonium salt. This salt couples with alpha-naphthylamine and produces a pink dye. No color means no nitrite.

    In experiment 2

    In acid, nitrite oxidizes iodide to iodine, and iodine with starch gives a blue color. The more nitrite there is, the faster iodine forms.

    2 NO₂⁻ + 2 I⁻ + 4 H⁺ → 2 NO + I₂ + 2 H₂O
    I₂ + starch → blue complex
